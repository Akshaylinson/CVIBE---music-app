What is CVIBE?

▶

Stream Everything

CVIBE connects to YouTube Music's catalogue — billions of songs, albums, playlists and podcasts — completely free and without ads.

■

No Account Required

Browse and play music without signing in. Log in with your Google account for personalised recommendations and playlist sync.

○

Every Platform

One app, four platforms. Android, Windows, macOS and Linux — all with the same clean interface and full feature set.

◆

Open Source

CVIBE is fully open source under GPL-3.0. No trackers, no telemetry, no hidden data collection in the FOSS build.

CVIBE is not affiliated with YouTube, Google or Spotify. It acts as a third-party client that streams publicly available content from YouTube Music. We do not host any audio or video files.

FAQ

Common questions

What is CVIBE?

CVIBE is a free, open-source music streaming client built by Codeless Technologies. It uses YouTube Music as its backend to give you access to a massive music catalogue without ads or subscriptions.

Is CVIBE free?

Yes. CVIBE is completely free with no ads, no premium tier and no hidden fees.

Does CVIBE require YouTube Music Premium?

No. Standard playback works without Premium. A YouTube Music Premium subscription unlocks higher audio quality (up to 256 kbps Opus/AAC).

Does CVIBE require a Google account?

No. You can browse and play music without signing in. Logging in with a Google account enables personalised recommendations, playlist sync and listening history.

Which platforms are supported?

Android (8.0+), Windows 10/11 (x64), macOS 15+ (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Linux (x64, AppImage).

Can I use CVIBE offline?

Yes. CVIBE supports song caching and offline playback. Smart downloads automatically cache the songs you play most.

Is CVIBE affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. CVIBE is an independent third-party client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to YouTube, Google or Spotify in any way.
